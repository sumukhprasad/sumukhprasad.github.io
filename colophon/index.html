Colophon

Principles

I ensure that things published on this site follow these guidelines:
  1. My words are not AI-generated. Nothing on this site (that is not clearly labeled as being AI-generated) is AI-generated.
  2. Content that is not mine is always attributed.
  3. Pages are (usually) plain HTML and CSS. Pages that requre JavaScript require JavaScript for either interactivity MathJax.

Details

Most of this site uses Lucida Grande/Lucida Sans, along with Georgia. No external webfonts. The site itself is published through Jekyll, but the blog/writing sections are published through Blogulator. Styling is done using SASS, but there's a bunch of style="..." attributes on things around here when I need to do some one-off styling thing.

This site is written almost exclusively in TextMate! (like most of my other projects as well) I actually keep a personal working fork of TextMate that I update occasionally when things break.

Iconography on this site is provided by Font Awesome by Dave Gandy.

Math on this site is rendered by MathJax.

Custom graphics you might see dotted around are handmade in Sketch.

The block print border on the left is a picture from the book "Block Prints from India for Textiles", a 1924 book by Albert Buell Lewis, accessed through the Internet Archive.
